Privacy Policy

nu. is a minimal daily journal. Here is exactly what happens with your data:

  • Journal entries (what you write, mood emoji, timestamps) are stored only on your device using local storage. They are never uploaded to any server we control and we cannot see them.
  • Purchases: the one-time Premium unlock is processed by Apple and by our payments provider, RevenueCat, to verify your purchase and restore it across devices. RevenueCat receives a device/purchase identifier and transaction data necessary to validate entitlements — see RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, or any analytics/advertising data.
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored journal entries. There is no account to delete.
