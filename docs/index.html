Privacy Policy

nu. is a minimal daily journal. Here is exactly what happens with your data:

  • Journal entries (what you write, mood emoji, timestamps) are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to any server we control and we cannot see them.
  • iPhone backups: a copy of your journal and app settings is kept in the app's storage on your device so it is included in your iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer), if you have backups turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple and stay under your control; we have no access to them.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. There is no push server. Replies you type into a reminder notification are saved on your device as journal entries.
  • Back up & restore (Premium) creates a file only when you choose to export one, and saves it wherever you pick (for example Files or iCloud Drive). Importing reads a file you choose. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Purchases: the one-time Premium unlock is processed by Apple and by our payments provider, RevenueCat, to verify your purchase and restore it across devices. RevenueCat receives a device/purchase identifier and transaction data necessary to validate entitlements — see RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, or any analytics/advertising data.
  • Deleting the app removes the journal from your device. Copies you made yourself (exported files, or iPhone backups made before you deleted the app) are not affected; you can delete them in Files or in your iPhone's iCloud settings. There is no account to delete.
